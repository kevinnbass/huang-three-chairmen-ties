Huang resignation · dated public records, Nov 2026 · draft

The three chairmen who summoned Huang: an Anthropic-funded group ran ads praising two of them on chip export controls, and Anthropic's employees gave their committees $113,000

Reps. Brian Mast, John Moolenaar and Rick Crawford signed the letters of Nov 5 and Nov 12, 2026 calling Nvidia's founder, Jensen Huang, before their committees. The letters followed a New York Times report of Oct 29, 2026, headlined "Nvidia Chief, Jensen Huang, Is Seen as Target of Chinese Spy Plot"; Nvidia told the Times his meetings with a customer's executive were "not unusual or unexpected". An article on X on Nov 12, 2026 alleged that "Anthropic's people overlap with the think tanks, ex-officials and forums behind this coverage." This figure lists every tie found, in the records searched, between each chairman and Anthropic or a group linked to it. A tie is a record with a date: the date of a gift, filing, hearing or page, not of when any relationship began or ended. None shows why anyone acted, and nothing here shows that any overlap caused or coordinated the coverage.

What is at stake between the two companies
The chip maker, now also releasing AI models
Nvidia
Chips. It sells AI chips, and two of the chairmen have bills on their export. Mast's, his committee says, "applies long-standing congressional oversight to exports of America's sensitive, military-enhancing AI chips"; it has passed his committee and no further, and Moolenaar's has not left committee. Mast, in the release of Jan 21, 2026: "Companies like Nvidia are requesting to sell millions of advanced AI chips, which are the cutting edge of warfare, to Chinese military companies like Alibaba and Tencent."
Now also AI models. It also releases open AI models, with data and tools. Its release of Dec 15, 2025: "NVIDIA is the first to release a collection of state-of-the-art open models, training datasets and reinforcement learning environments and libraries"; the same release describes developers using open models alongside "proprietary models for state-of-the-art reasoning". A trade report of Oct 16, 2026 says its newest models show it "reinforcing its own GPU infrastructure while supporting the open source community."
On the Senate's bill. TechSpot, Sep 4, 2025: "Nvidia has since posted a statement opposing the GAIN AI Act. The company says it is comparable to the Biden-era AI Diffusion Rule that was rescinded in May, and part of an attempt to overturn Trump's AI Action Plan". It also reported Nvidia's statement "that H20 sales have not reduced its supply of H100, H200, or Blackwell GPUs". Of that rule Huang had said, as Fortune quoted him on May 2, 2025: "the U.S. semiconductor industry is being pushed out of China, the world's largest market."
The AI model maker, now building a chip-design team
Anthropic
AI models. It makes the Claude AI models and runs them on other companies' chips: "Currently, Anthropic uses a large number of chips, including Nvidia and AMD GPUs, Google TPUs, and Amazon Trainium hardware" (Data Center Dynamics, Aug 6, 2026).
Now also a chip-design team. In Aug 2026 its spokesperson confirmed "that the company was building an in-house silicon team to design custom chips for Claude", calling it "the latest step in our multi-chip approach" and saying the company would continue deploying a "diversified hardware stack". The report is of a design team: it does not describe finished chips, or chips for sale.
On export controls. It asks for tighter limits on chip exports. Its co-founder to Moolenaar's committee on Jun 25, 2025, before either House bill was introduced: the government should be "maintaining and strengthening export controls of advanced semiconductors to China". That is its general position, not a statement on either bill.
Its own account. Announcing its first gift to Public First Action on Feb 12, 2026, it said of the policies it backs: "Nor are they for the benefit of Anthropic as an AI developer—effective AI governance means more scrutiny of companies like ours, not less." That is the company's statement; no record here tests it.
Where the two have met in public
Clash, and partnership
May 1, 2025. After Anthropic argued for tighter controls and described chip smuggling, an Nvidia spokesperson said American firms should "rise to the challenge, rather than tell tall tales that large, heavy, and sensitive electronics are somehow smuggled in 'baby bumps' or 'alongside live lobsters,'" and: "America cannot manipulate regulators to capture victory in AI."
Anthropic's spokesperson replied: "National security is bigger than the interest of any single company. Anthropic stands by its recently filed public submission in support of strong and balanced export controls".
Also partners. Reported on Nov 10, 2026: Anthropic said seven companies, Nvidia among them, had agreed to buy $26 billion of its stock between them, in a private sale at the price of its public offering. Nvidia's own share is not given. Anthropic also runs Claude partly on Nvidia chips.

Reading it: On the left, the first band says what each company makes and where the two have clashed; the key says once how each group connects to Anthropic; the table and the timeline put the three chairmen side by side. On the right, each panel lists its records by that link. An employee's gift is that person's own money: the FEC line shows the employer the donor wrote down. A lobbyist's gift is reported by the lobbyist as his or her own; one line is a campaign committee's contribution that a lobbyist's report lists, and the panel says so. Donors and lobbyists are counted here, not named; the one exception is Anthropic's chief executive, whose gift to a super PAC is in the key. How it was made: The employees' gift lines come from the FEC's bulk file of itemised individual contributions for the 2026 cycle, downloaded Nov 13, 2026 (it carries whole dollars), plus the lines its API holds and the file does not yet (nearly all dated Jul to Sep 2026): 80 of them. For 8 more lines the API's later filing replaces the file's copy. Nine Anthropic lines to Banks for Senate are counted from a copy of the committee's report saved in Sep 2026: its amended report leaves the employer blank. A line counts if an individual gave the company as employer to a committee tied to a sitting member, not as a memo line; a joint committee's later allocation is not counted again. Committees tied to a member are his campaign committee, plus joint and leadership committees an earlier evidence set keyed to him; a gift to a committee not so keyed is missed. Lobbyists' gifts are from the contribution reports lobbyists and their firms file with the Senate (gifts dated Jan 2024 to Jun 2026): a gift counts if it names the chairman and the firm had a lobbying registration for the client on that day. A lobbyist is "listed" for a client from the first quarter a filing names him or her for it to 90 days after the last. The same rule is applied to Anthropic's firms and the firms of the groups in the key. Trade groups' PACs are from the FEC's file of committees' gifts to candidates, and each group's members from its own page. Campaign totals are from the FEC's summary file; PAC money from the committees' FEC filings through Sep 30, 2026; lobbying reports through the third quarter of 2026; grant totals are sums of the awards Open Philanthropy lists, and for Horizon a foundation's tax filing; directors are from tax returns; fellows, staff and witnesses are from the programme's, the groups' and Congress's own pages. The ads are from Google's political-ads report and the ad pages it links to; the words on screen were read from frames of each video, and the Census Bureau's table matches the Florida ad's zip codes to the district.
